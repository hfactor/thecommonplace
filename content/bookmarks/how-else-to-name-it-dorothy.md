[Dorothy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dorothy_(film)) is a wonderful film with stunning music from Ilaiyaraaja. Thanks to [@aswvee](https://www.instagram.com/aswvee/), I came to know about Ilaiyaraaja’s 1986 album [How to Name It?](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/How_to_Name_It%3F), from which they reused "Do Anything" as the BGM for a particular scene and named it "How Else to Name It". A remake of a 40-year-old masterpiece. Raaja Raaja thaan!
