Went to an [A. R. Rahman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A._R._Rahman) concert. Good, but his music is really about the engineering, and the live felt like a draft that reminds you of the effects and effort in the released versions. A masterclass with homework. YouTube Music is full of covers and bad audio, so I went to the remastered versions by [Sreejesh](https://film-mixing.com/), who is an even bigger fan of [H. Sridhar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/H._Sridhar) and runs [The Mastering Project](https://www.youtube.com/@TheMasteringProject/). Thanks again, Sreejesh!
