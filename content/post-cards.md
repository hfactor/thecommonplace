A postcard from an Indian post office still costs 50 paise. I can’t think of a better souvenir, so wherever I go, I send a few to friends. I started this a decade ago, took a break, and recently picked it up again. I usually carry a few postcards and send them from whichever post office I happen to find in India, to people who probably weren’t expecting one. Abroad, fewer, depending on cost and postal reliability.

Want one? Send me your postal address by email or on social media, and let me know when it arrives. I’m happy to receive postcards too, so just ask for my address.
