Added [Scenes](/scenes/) to the site, a new corner for things that are better seen than written about. More categories are on the way, and soon "Everything" will actually mean everything 😉

[The Many Lives of an Aeropress](/scenes/the-many-lives-of-an-aeropress/) happened, and it went better than I hoped. About twenty people showed up at Cafe Addict in Kochi, and strangers turned into collaborators in no time, comparing grinds, swapping presses and laughing at their first attempts. Watching someone taste their own cup and realise a tiny change moved the whole flavour is the part I show up for.
