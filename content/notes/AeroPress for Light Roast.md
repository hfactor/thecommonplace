| | |
|---|---|
| Coffee | 14g light roast, medium-fine grind |
| Water | 200ml at 85°C to 88°C |
| Position | Standard |
| Filter | 2 paper filters, rinsed |
| Beans | Jumping Ant |
| Target | 160ml |

1. Set up standard, upright, on your cup.
2. Add the coffee.
3. Pour in 100ml water, insert the plunger slightly to seal.
4. Wait 60 seconds.
5. Pull the plunger out, stir the slurry 2 to 3 times.
6. Pour in the remaining 100ml, reinsert the plunger.
7. Press very gently over about 30 seconds.
