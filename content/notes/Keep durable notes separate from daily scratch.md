---
title: "Keep durable notes separate from daily scratch"
date: 2026-03-05
categories: ["Analog Journaling"]
categories_weight: 7
---
Not everything belongs in the notebook. Ideas, questions, observations, things worth coming back to, those go in. Day to day scheduling and quick meeting notes are better off on loose paper you can throw away later. Mixing the two just makes the notebook harder to review.
