title: "Keep durable notes separate from daily scratch"
date: 2026-03-05

---
Daily scratch stays out of the notebook these days. Long meetings, quarter planning workshops, even a single day of [time blocking](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eff9h1WYxSo), all go on separate paper, stapled back in later or just thrown out.

Too much to-do turns a notebook into a reminder app instead of a journal, and there's rarely anything in a time block worth reviewing later, the point was just getting it done.

Cheap [writing blocks](https://link.amazon/B01s2tQPx) or [A4 notepads](https://link.amazon/B0diPCtss) handle this kind of quick writing, nothing precious about them.
