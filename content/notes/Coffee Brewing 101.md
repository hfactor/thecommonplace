Manual brewing is simply making coffee yourself and having control over how you make it. You decide the coffee, grind size, water, temperature and brewing time, and each of these can change how the final cup tastes. 

There are many ways to brew coffee manually. [[Pour Over]], [[AeroPress]], [[French Press]], [[South Indian Filter]] and [[Moka Pot]] all work differently and produce very different cups. That’s part of the fun. You can start with a simple recipe and slowly figure out what works for you.
