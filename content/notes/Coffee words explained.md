| Word       | Meaning                                                                      |
| ---------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Aroma      | The smell of the coffee.                                                     |
| Flavor     | Taste and smell together.                                                    |
| Acidity    | A bright, fruit-like quality (Think apple). Nothing to do with your stomach. |
| Sourness   | Sharp and unpleasant, usually a sign of under-extraction.                    |
| Bitterness | Tastes like dark chocolate or roasted food.                                  |
| Body       | How heavy the coffee feels, from tea-like to syrup-like.                     |
| Mouthfeel  | The physical sensation of the liquid: smooth, creamy, dry, watery.           |
| Clarity    | How clearly you can pick out individual flavors.                             |
| Finish     | What's left in your mouth after swallowing.                                  |
| Balance    | How well sweetness, acidity, and bitterness sit together.                    |
| Clean cup  | No sediment, no muddiness, no harshness.                                     |
| Complexity | Several distinct flavors that keep shifting as you drink.                    |
| Chicory    | A roasted root mixed into traditional South Indian coffee powders.           |
