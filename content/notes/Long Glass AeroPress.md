| | |
|---|---|
| Coffee | 16g medium-dark or dark roast, medium-fine grind |
| Water | 360ml at 85°C to 88°C, including dilution |
| Position | Standard |
| Filter | 1 paper filter, rinsed |
| Beans | Tall Dark Handsome, Loris, Liquid Luck, Julien Peak Forest |
| Target | 300ml+ |

1. Add the coffee to an upright AeroPress.
2. Pour 30ml water, bloom for 30 seconds.
3. Add 150ml hot water.
4. Steep for 90 seconds.
5. Press gently over about 30 seconds, for a total brew time of 2:00 to 2:30.
6. Add 180ml hot water directly to the concentrate to dilute.
