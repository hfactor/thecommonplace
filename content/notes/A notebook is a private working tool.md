Bullet journaling on social media has made a lot of people feel like their notebook needs to look good. Hand lettering, color coding, neat spreads. That's a different activity from actually using a notebook to think. A notebook that's doing its job is usually messy, and that's fine, since nobody else needs to see it. [Owl Paper has a good take on functional, unpolished layouts](https://owlpaper.co/three-simple-functional-daily-layouts-to-bullet-journal/) as their own kind of honest.
