---
title: "An index makes a single notebook searchable"
date: 2026-03-05
categories: ["Analog Journaling"]
categories_weight: 6
---
Once everything lives in one notebook, finding an old note again gets harder. Numbering the pages and keeping a running index at the front solves most of that, at least for [[Keep durable notes separate from daily scratch]]. It takes a minute to maintain and saves a lot of flipping through old pages later. [John Locke worked out a formal indexing method for commonplace books back in 1685](https://publicdomainreview.org/collection/john-lockes-method-for-common-place-books-1685/), and it still holds up.
