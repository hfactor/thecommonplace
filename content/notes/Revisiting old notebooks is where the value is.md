---
title: "Revisiting old notebooks is where the value is"
date: 2026-03-05
categories: ["Analog Journaling"]
categories_weight: 11
---
Filling notebooks and putting them on a shelf isn't really note-taking, it's just archiving. The actual value shows up when you go back and read what you wrote. Set aside time to read through old notebooks, cross off what's been dealt with, and sit with questions you haven't answered yet. That's usually where the better connections show up.
