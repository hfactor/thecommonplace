Cooler water than usual here, which keeps the bitterness down. This uses the same idea as the [[South Indian Filter Recipe]], just with an AeroPress instead of the traditional filter.

| | |
|---|---|
| Coffee | 13g South Indian coffee powder, roughly 4:1 Robusta to chicory |
| Water | 210ml at 80°C |
| Position | Standard |
| Filter | 2 paper filters, rinsed |
| Target | 180ml |

1. Add the coffee to an upright AeroPress.
2. Pour 60ml water at 80°C, bloom for 30 seconds.
3. Add the remaining 150ml, insert the plunger to hold the water back.
4. Wait 30 seconds.
5. Press gently over 20 to 30 seconds, stopping just before the hiss.
