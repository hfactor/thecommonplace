title: "How to taste a cup"
date: 2026-02-20

---
Tasting is personal, and mostly just about noticing what's actually in the cup rather than reaching for the "right" word.

**Smell** it before drinking. Fruit, nuts, spice, chocolate, wood, whatever comes to mind is fine.

**Sip** a small amount and let it spread across the tongue. Slurping helps, since it pushes aroma up into the nose.

**Feel** the weight and texture. Light like tea, or heavy like milk. Smooth, creamy, dry, watery.

**Swallow**, then notice what's left. Does it fade fast, or does something sweet linger.

**Let it cool.** Hot coffee tends to hide flavor. Acidity, sweetness, and complexity all get easier to notice as it cools down.
