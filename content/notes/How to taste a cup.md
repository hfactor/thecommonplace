Tasting is personal. Smell the coffee, take a sip, and notice the flavour, texture, and what lingers after you swallow. Let it cool too, as sweetness, acidity, and other flavors become easier to notice.

The [SCA Coffee Taster’s Flavor Wheel](https://notbadcoffee.com/flavor-wheel-en/) is a tool to help find words for what you taste, not a standard to match. Tasting notes are personal perceptions, not absolute descriptions, and they may differ from person to person.
