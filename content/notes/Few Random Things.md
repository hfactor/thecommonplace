1. **Focus on your taste preference.**  Don’t get too caught up in what someone else says is a good cup of coffee. If you like it, that’s what matters. You will slowly figure out what kind of coffee, roast, grind and brewing style you enjoy.
    
2. **Robusta is not bad coffee.**  There is a lot of talk about Arabica being better and Robusta being inferior. It is not that simple. Robusta can make a very good cup. Try different coffees and decide based on what you like.
    
3. **Reuse your Aeropress filter.**  You don’t have to throw away the paper filter after every cup. Rinse it properly and you can reuse it a few times. PS: I have tried a steel filter with the Aeropress, but it never worked for me.
    
4. **Be ready to experiment.**  There is no need to follow a recipe exactly every time. Change the grind size, water temperature, coffee-to-water ratio or brewing time and see what happens. Small changes can make a noticeable difference.
    
5. **Do have coffee earlier, if that affects sleep.**  If coffee affects your sleep, don’t drink it late just because you want another cup. Have it earlier in the day.
    
6. **Note down your experiments.**  When you are trying different coffees and recipes, write it down. A notebook is enough.
