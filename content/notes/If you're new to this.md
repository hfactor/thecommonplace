**Sugar and milk.** Try dropping them for a while, even if it feels odd at first. It's a decent way to notice what the coffee actually tastes like.

**Chicory.** Worth moving away from, toward 100% coffee, Arabica if you can find it. Chicory has a strong aroma that tends to sit over the coffee's own smell and flavor.

**Freshness.** Roasted beans don't hold for long, and ground coffee fades within a couple of weeks. Buying smaller batches and keeping them dry and airtight makes a real difference.

**Water.** Water that tastes fine on its own is usually good enough. If you want a number, pH around 7 or TDS between 75 and 250 ppm.

**Patience.** Brewing rewards it. There's a saying for this: "Patience mukhyam aanu."
