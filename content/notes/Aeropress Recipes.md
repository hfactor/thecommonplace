##### Standard / Upright
1. [Alan Adler](https://aeromatic.app/recipes/alan-adler-aeropress-recipe), Classic Black Coffee
2. [James Hoffmann](https://aeromatic.app/recipes/james-hoffmann-aeropress-recipe), Easy to follow, great result
3. [Tim Wendelboe](https://aeromatic.app/recipes/tim-wendelboe-aeropress-recipe) Master's Coffee, 14gm, 1:14 ratio. [Worth Watching](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7JISZNixkeA)
##### Inverted
1. [Carlo Graf Bülow](https://aeromatic.app/recipes/wac-2023-runner-up-aeropress-recipe), when you are in the mood to stir vigorously 32 times. Works great between bad meetings. 
2. [For Light Coffee](https://aeromatic.app/recipes/lance-hedrick-light-roast-aeropress-recipe), Lance Hedrick's 1:6 method (20gm) is a good extraction technique.
