Coffee and water are the two essentials. About 98% of your cup is water.  Start with clean RO water. For a simple baseline, aim for a pH around 7 and TDS between 75–200.

For coffee, fresher is better. Buy good-quality roasted beans, grind them as needed, and store them dry and airtight. Buy smaller batches, especially for pre-ground coffee.

*If you are in South India, choose coffee without [[chicory]] if you want to taste the coffee itself. Start without sugar. It’s the easiest way to notice its aroma and flavor.*

There are different ways to make coffee. The method you use affects how the water and coffee interact, which changes the taste and texture of the cup.
