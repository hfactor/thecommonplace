date: 2026-03-05

---
A pocket notebook doesn't have to hold everything forever. It's a good place to sketch out an idea before it's ready for anywhere else, in keeping with how [[Paper slows thinking]] works as a first pass. Once something's worked out enough, it can move into a digital tool, a bookmark, a document, wherever it actually needs to live and be found again.
