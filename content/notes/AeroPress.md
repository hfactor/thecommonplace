AeroPress is a brewing method that combines immersion brewing with pressure. You steep ground coffee in hot water inside the AeroPress, then press the brewed coffee through a paper filter and into your cup. You need an [AeroPress](https://link.amazon/B0gyzgIzh) or it's travel friendly version [AeroPress Go](https://brewinggadgets.in/products/aeropress-go-travel-coffee-press) and a [paper filter](https://link.amazon/B0aIETYlh). Unlike pour-over, the AeroPress allows the coffee to steep before you press it through the filter. 

There are many ways to brew with an AeroPress, but the clearest difference is how you position it: **standard** or **inverted**. In the standard method, it sits on your cup and the coffee is pressed straight through the filter. In the inverted method, it is turned upside down while the coffee steeps, so it doesn't drain before you're ready to press.

|          |                 |
| -------- | --------------- |
| Coffee   | 15g             |
| Grind    | Medium-fine     |
| Water    | 240ml at 85°C   |
| Time     | About 2 minutes |
| Position | Standard        |

1. Place a paper filter in the filter cap and rinse it with hot water. Discard the rinse water.
2. Attach the filter cap and place the AeroPress **upright on your cup**.
3. Add **15g of coffee**.
4. Pour **240ml of water at 85°C** over the coffee, making sure all the grounds are wet.
5. Stir gently and let the coffee steep.
6. At around **2 minutes**, press the plunger down slowly and steadily.
7. Remove the AeroPress and enjoy.
