---
title: "Paper slows thinking"
date: 2026-03-05
categories: ["Analog Journaling"]
categories_weight: 1
---
Writing by hand adds a bit of friction that typing doesn't have, and that friction seems to matter. Digital tools make it easy to dump thoughts quickly. A pen on paper slows things down just enough to make you think about what you're actually trying to say before it hits the page. It works well as a staging ground, somewhere thoughts sit and settle before they're ready to move into a digital system or get typed up properly. [Austin Kleon splits his desk into physical and digital halves](https://austinkleon.com/gear/) for something close to this reason.
