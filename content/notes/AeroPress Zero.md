No scale needed, which makes it the one to reach for while travelling.

| | |
|---|---|
| Coffee | One level AeroPress scoop |
| Roast / grind | Medium roast, medium-fine, like sand |
| Position | Inverted |
| Filter | 2 paper filters |
| Beans | Say Hello to Mellow |

1. Boil water, let it cool slightly.
2. Rinse the plunger, chamber, and filter cap with hot water.
3. Fit the plunger into the chamber and stand it inverted.
4. Add the coffee.
5. Pour a splash of water to wet the grounds, let it bloom.
6. Stir twice, left to right, then top to bottom.
7. Fill the chamber to the top with the rest of the water.
8. Screw the filter cap on.
9. Flip onto your cup and press gently until you hear a hiss.
10. Let it settle a minute, then drink as is or dilute with water or ice.
