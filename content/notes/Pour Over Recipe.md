Hot water poured over grounds sitting in a paper filter. Gravity does most of the work, and the paper holds back sediment and oils, which tends to give a cleaner, clearer cup than most other methods.

Gear that helps: a Hario V60 filter and brewer, a gooseneck kettle.

| | |
|---|---|
| Coffee | 15g |
| Water | 250g |
| Temperature | 92°C to 96°C |
| Ratio | 1:16.7 |
| Grind | Medium-fine to medium, like sand |
| Time | 2:30 to 3:30 |

1. Rinse the paper filter with hot water, discard the rinse.
2. Add the coffee, shake gently to level the bed.
3. Start a timer.
4. Pour 30 to 45g of water to wet all the grounds.
5. Wait 30 to 45 seconds for the coffee to bloom.
6. Pour the rest slowly, in gentle circles, up to 250g.
7. Let it drain fully.
8. Taste, and adjust next time if it's off.
