Coffee and water steep together, then get pushed through a paper filter with a plunger. It's forgiving, travel-friendly, and there's more than one reasonable way to use it, so a few recipes are worth keeping around. [Aeroprecipe](https://aeroprecipe.com/) and the [Aeromatic App](https://aeromatic.app/) are both good places to find more, and [James Hoffmann's Ultimate AeroPress Recipe](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j6VlT_jUVPc) is a solid starting point if you're new to it.

A few versions worth trying: [[AeroPress Zero]], [[AeroPress for Light Roast]], [[V60-Style AeroPress]], [[Long Glass AeroPress]], and [[South Indian Filter with AeroPress]].
