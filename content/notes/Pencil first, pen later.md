---
title: "Pencil first, pen later"
date: 2026-03-05
categories: ["Analog Journaling"]
categories_weight: 10
---
Writing in pencil first keeps things low pressure. Mistakes don't feel permanent, so it's easier to write quickly and loosely. Going over it later in pen works like a review pass, crossing out what's done, striking through what's no longer relevant, adding a counter-argument next to something you got wrong the first time.
