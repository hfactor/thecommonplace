Coffee sits in hot water for several minutes. A metal mesh plunger then separates the brew from the grounds, letting the oils through with it. There's no pouring technique to get right here, which is most of the appeal, and [James Hoffmann's take on the technique](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=st571DYYTR8) is worth watching if you want to see it done well.
