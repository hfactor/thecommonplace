Keeping separate notebooks for work, personal thoughts, and projects tends to fall apart. One notebook for everything is messier, but it's also more likely to actually get used. A small notebook, something like 32 pages, fills up in a month or so, which means you finish it, archive it, and start clean without much ceremony. This isn't a new idea. [Commonplacing, keeping one book for everything worth keeping, goes back centuries](https://fortelabs.com/blog/commonplace-books-creative-note-taking-through-history/), through people like Milton and Jefferson.
