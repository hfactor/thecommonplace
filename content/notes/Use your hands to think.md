---
title: Use your hands to think
date: 2021-06-22
---

Physical tools change how ideas form. Paper and pen, a whiteboard, a printout with a marker, they slow the process down in a way that's useful. Mistakes can't be undone, you have to cross out, redraw, reconsider, and that friction is part of the thinking.
