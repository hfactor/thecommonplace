Almost everything gets noted down, then most of it gets cancelled. It's less about capturing the right things upfront and more about reviewing later and cutting hard.

The margins do the tracking. Something moved to [Raindrop](https://raindrop.io/), filed into an Obsidian note, or simply done gets marked and struck out, in both the index and the entry, so it's obvious at a glance what's still open.
