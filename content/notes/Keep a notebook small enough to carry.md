---
title: "Keep a notebook small enough to carry"
date: 2026-03-05
categories: ["Analog Journaling"]
categories_weight: 5
---
The best notebook is the one that's actually with you. Something pocket sized, an A6 or close to it, fits in a bag or a pocket without a second thought, so it's there when a thought is worth catching. That immediacy matters more than most features a notebook could have. [Austin Kleon calls his pocket notebook his least formal one](https://austinkleon.com/2025/06/05/a-peek-inside-my-pocket-notebook/), a place for raw, unfiltered thinking that keeps everything else moving.
