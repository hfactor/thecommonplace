The best notebook is the one that's actually with you. Something pocket sized, an A6 or close to it, fits in a bag or a pocket without a second thought, so it's there when a thought is worth catching.

A plain [grid notebook](https://link.amazon/B0b2XQ3v1) does the job, 32 pages, barely ₹30 a book as of July 2026, with a [colored cover version](https://link.amazon/B08lpEQjl) too if plain isn't your thing. Both are small enough to disappear into a pocket.

A few others came before these. [Neorah's Petite](https://link.amazon/B0j7bN3A8) is a longer book, good for writing more per sitting. [Accuprints A6](https://link.amazon/B00GY9wsq) is hardbound, for something sturdier.
