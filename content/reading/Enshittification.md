Enshittification is the answer to a question you already knew you were asking. [Cory Doctorow](https://craphound.com/) lays out simple facts and timelines, but is razor clear on exactly what is happening to the platforms we use. Brutal, witty, borderline fear-mongering, except it's all true. Engaging, often funny writing, enough that I want to read more of his work.
