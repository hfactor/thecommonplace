Went into this Tamil audiobook with zero context, first time doing that, and it held up well. The book is beautiful and conversational, moves at a slow pace, no fancy twists, just close to real life. The way the writer describes a scene feels like a painting, you can feel like you're sitting right there in that village tea shop or inside the lorry. Reminded me of [Meyyazhagan](https://www.netflix.com/in/title/81713677) a lot. [Its book version](https://link.amazon/B03jug2Zq) is out now, waiting on the audiobook for that one, *or I'll go fail at reading Tamil again, like I always do.*
