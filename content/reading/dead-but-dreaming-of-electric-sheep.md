I don’t know who recommended this book, but I could kill them. I have been reading about AI pessimism, AI slop and questions about the future, and I did not expect dark humour to turn into AI horror. We have read fiction like this before, but this one is too close to reality, and the second-person narration makes it hit closer than it should.
